Cybersecurity engineer, security researcher, and senior DevOps/cloud infrastructure engineer with 15+ years of experience across penetration testing, vulnerability research, intrusion detection, applied AI for security monitoring, and large-scale cloud & HPC infrastructure. PhD in Cybersecurity from Goethe University Frankfurt, in collaboration with CERN. Published researcher with 200+ peer-reviewed papers and 6 assigned CVEs, including a Microsoft-acknowledged memory-corruption vulnerability (MS13-056).

Experienced across the full security and infrastructure lifecycle: ethical hacking and source code audits for banks and ISPs, five years building AWS/Kubernetes/Terraform infrastructure for blockchain products at Edenia, seven years embedded in the ALICE experiment at CERN managing critical cluster infrastructure, and founding a security startup. Comfortable working close to the metal (exploit development, fuzzing) and at the systems level (cloud infrastructure, observability, DevSecOps).

Currently Co-Founder & CEO of [Sakundi](https://sakundi.io), a security, privacy, and digital identity startup, and a part-time professor teaching blockchain security and infrastructure/DevOps practices in the Cybersecurity Master's program at the Costa Rica Institute of Technology (TEC).

Selected projects
======
* **[Tikuna](https://github.com/sakundi/tikuna)** — AI-based security monitoring system for the Ethereum P2P network (nodes & validators), funded by an Ethereum Foundation Academic Research grant.
* **[Arhuaco](https://github.com/kuronosec/arhuaco)** — PhD thesis project at CERN: deep learning and isolation-based intrusion detection for distributed high-throughput computing.
* **[Zikuani](https://github.com/kuronosec/zikuani)** — Privacy-preserving identity verification system using zero-knowledge proofs. Backed by ZK Bankai and Polygon; DIF 2025 hackathon winner.
* **[ZK Voto Digital](https://github.com/kuronosec/zk-voto-digital)** — Secure voting system using Zikuani to verify voter eligibility (humanity, citizenship, age) without exposing personal identity.
